# Ispra 2019

This is the presentation given at the *Neutron and Muons for Magnetism 2019*
SISN Advanced School in Ispra, 2019.

## Programme

### Part 1
Introduction to linear spin-wave theory, SpinW and basic examples.

### Part 2
Magnetic ordering, Horace and more complex examples.

- Duc Le — STFC Harwell
